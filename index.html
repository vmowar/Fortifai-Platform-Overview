01 · The enterprise blind spot
Much of the enterprise data (ERP, CRM, T&E, DMS or unstrictured) is never analysed. Traditional risk mitigation methods like audits remain periodic, manual and sample-based. This leaves a structural blind spot, where an estimated 3% to 5% of revenue can leak out.
Example:Procurement & Supply Chain Risk

Un-Captured Commodity Price Drops & Middleman Markups

Suppliers push immediate price hikes when raw material indices rise, but keep contract prices flat when indices fall 10%–15%. Meanwhile, plants unknowingly buy components through zero-capacity traders at a 20%–30% markup instead of directly from the OEM.

Example:Operational & Control Bypass Risk

Sub-Tolerance Overbilling & Split Orders

Vendors systematically bill above PO price (just beneath ERP tolerance block), buyers split requisitions to bypass competitive bidding and approval gates, or retroactive price increases are keyed after goods are received.

Example:Finance & Working Capital Risk

Early Disbursements & Period-Close Exceptions

Invoices are paid weeks ahead of contractual due dates, creating working-capital drag, early-settlement discounts go uncaptured, and manual period-end journal entries create balance-sheet close bottlenecks.

Example:Sales, Trade & Third-Party Risk

Revenue Leakage, Channel Stuffing & Compliance Exposure

Quarter-end sales spikes reverse via post-period credit memos, unapproved discounts erode gross margin, customer credit limits are breached, and post-clearance trade or third-party integrity risks go undetected.
